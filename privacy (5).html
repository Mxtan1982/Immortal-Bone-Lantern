1. Introduction

MMLucky Team ("we", "us", or "our") provides Immortal Bone Lantern, a mobile app for reading fiction in multiple languages and listening to chapters using text-to-speech ("TTS"). This Privacy Policy explains what information may be handled when you use the app, why it is used, which third parties may process it, and your choices.

2. Information handled by the app

Reading preferences on your device: The app stores settings such as reading progress, selected language, bookmarks, appearance and font choices, and some ad-display timing preferences locally on your device. These settings support reading and app operation. Based on the current app design, we do not operate an account service or separately upload these reading preferences to our own server.

Communications: If you email us for support or privacy questions, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. We use it to reply and handle your request.

Advertising data: The app integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). When ads are enabled, Google and its advertising technology partners may automatically collect or receive information including IP address (which may indicate approximate location), advertising or other device identifiers, app/ad interactions and usage events, and diagnostics such as performance or crash-related information. This information is processed for ad delivery, measurement, security, fraud prevention, and, where allowed, ad personalization. Advertising data practices may differ depending on your device settings, location, consent choices, and Google services.

3. Advertising and your choices

Immortal Bone Lantern may display banner and interstitial advertisements powered by Google AdMob. Where required, the app requests advertising privacy choices before requesting ads. If a privacy-options control is available within the app, you can use it to review or change eligible choices. You can also manage or reset your Android advertising identifier through your device's privacy settings.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and My Ad Center help.

4. Text-to-speech and language support

Listening to chapters uses the device's installed TTS engine or voice service. The app passes the chapter text and selected language or voice settings to that engine for speech generation. Depending on the voice provider and installed voice, processing may occur on the device or through the provider's online service. We do not record your microphone or collect audio recordings as part of this reading feature. Refer to the privacy terms of the TTS engine installed on your device for its processing and retention practices.

5. Permissions

The app uses internet/network-state access to load advertisements and related services. Advertising-related identifiers may be accessed by the advertising SDK as permitted by your Android version, settings, and applicable consent. Normal chapter reading does not require access to your camera, contacts, or precise GPS location.

6. Sharing and third-party services

We do not run a service that sells your reading history. Third-party services integrated into the app may process information as described above:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK: advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and associated processing under Google's policies.
  • Device TTS engine or voice provider: processing of text submitted for voice playback in accordance with the provider's policies.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or to respond to lawful requests.

7. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored bookmarks and reading settings remain on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, subject to your device's backup or restore settings. Support emails may be retained as needed to respond, maintain records, or meet legal obligations; you can request deletion of information you provided by contacting us at the address below. Google and other third-party service providers maintain their own retention and deletion practices, which we do not control.

8. Security

We seek to minimize the information we handle directly. Locally stored preferences rely on protections offered by your device and operating system. Communications with Google advertising services use the security measures provided by those services, including encryption in transit as described by Google. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Children

The app is a fiction-reading product and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information through an app account. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us directly, contact us so we can review the request.

10. International users

The app offers content in multiple languages. Third-party advertising and speech services may process data in countries other than your own, subject to their respective practices and applicable safeguards. Users in regions with additional privacy rights can contact us using the details below, and may use the advertising privacy options made available in the app where applicable.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, third-party services, or legal obligations change. We will publish the revised version on this page and update the effective date above.
